RAG Sales Bot

Python · Team of 2

Built an AI sales assistant from scratch that answers questions using a company's own documents. The system parses source files, turns them into embeddings, stores them in a ChromaDB vector database, and retrieves the most relevant passages to ground each answer. We also designed evaluation metrics to measure answer quality and managed the codebase with a Git/GitHub workflow.
